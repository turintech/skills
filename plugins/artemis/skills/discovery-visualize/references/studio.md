# The chart studio: what the page draws

Read this when you change the studio or answer a question about one of its cards. `SKILL.md` has the commands; this file is what the page does with a snapshot. The page draws the snapshot and decides nothing the collector decides (see [data-contract.md](data-contract.md)): falcon's verdicts and intervals, the collector's Best, gate, size cap, budget metric, drawable trade-off, running best and Pareto fronts. Leave the template's charts and wording alone: `check_report.py --studio` and the tests hold the page to them.

## Worked out on this page

Whatever the page works out itself, rather than reads from Artemis, says so in a card's ⓘ in one wording, "worked out on this page from Artemis's numbers" (`here()` in `assets/studio-core.js`); the report kit uses the same words. The studio shows no chip or header line for it: a card that picks or converts says what in its ⓘ only (the size cap and gate, the times-better view, Best on one metric alone). The ranked order and Top, the new leaders and every Pareto front are read from the collector, so they get none. The static report kit keeps its labels.

## Layout

- **Top of the page:** the project and run title (the page's one `<h1>`), with the page's controls (project, session, theme, Download PDF, Reset), then straight into the chart. A first visit opens dark (`DEFAULT_THEME`); a stored Theme choice wins, and the switch still offers Auto, Light and Dark. A script at the top of `<head>` sets the theme before the first paint, and holds `PREFIX` and `DEFAULT_THEME` for the page. Print is always light.
- **Chart rail:** one chart at a time, picked in a rail flush with the content's right edge (above it under 760px, scrolling sideways), as in the platform's Graphs tab. Each item has an inline icon of its chart type (drawn in `currentColor`, `--accent` when open; no icon font or CDN) and its name; a button at the top (`aria-expanded`) folds the rail to icons only, each keeping its name as its label and tooltip. It opens expanded; a fold is remembered only once the reader folds it. One item per view with data, in this order (`railViews` in studio-core): Discovery sessions (a project), Best against the original, Compare all versions (the change card), Trade-off (the trade-off card, or the Pareto card where it can't be drawn), Over time (a steered run only), Box plots (when they can be drawn), AI Metrics, then All versions, last. The page always opens on Best against the original. The open item is `aria-current`, each item is a button with a visible focus ring, and every view stays drawn (hidden until picked), so selections and picks carry across views. A switch fades the open view out (`--t-hover`) and eases the next in (`--t-highlight`, `--ease-enter`), with the stage held at its height meanwhile so nothing jumps; the revealed card is drawn afresh, so its bars grow and points fade in again. Reduced motion and print switch at once. A view is laid out as the rail reveals it, and again once it has settled. Print and Download PDF show every view in order, laid out first (`beforeprint`). The checker sees every view at once, then walks the rail one view at a time, as a reader does.
- **Page notes:** what is about the whole page, not about Best (runs left out, values Artemis sent as non-numbers, a session that is gone, each stored-direction warning), under the charts and above the footer, whichever view is open.
- **Under chart 1 (the summary):** every fact about Best against the original, in that view under its chart, in this order: the hero, the headline (an `<h2>`), then the Conclusion box: how Best was chosen, in one line ("Best = highest Composite score across all metrics.", or, when Artemis gives no Best or its Best has no change on the lead metric, a line saying so and that the page leads with the Top on that metric; with a CLI before 1.1.20 it says to update the CLI to see Artemis's Best). Only Artemis's Composite pick is ever called Best: otherwise the pick is "Top" on every card, chart 1's legend says "Top on <metric>", and chart 1's ⓘ says the page picked it because Artemis gives no Best on it. Then each metric whose own best is another version: 3 by name (by importance, then the size of that version's gain, then the lead order), the rest folded behind a closed "and N more". The hero gives Best's change on the lead metric: a × only beside falcon's better or worse (converted from falcon's % change, which the hero's own ⓘ says), else the % change. It follows chart 1's rule: no interval or verdict; the summary's Conclusion has them.
- **Cards, in order:** Best against the original, the simplest chart: each panel a plain bar of the original (neutral) and the Best (always Artemis purple, `--accent`; the panel's one phrase carries the sign, and verdict colours are for chart 2 on), with the change as one plain phrase only, "40.5% better than the original (lower is better)" (`betterPhrase` in studio-core, the same words in every Conclusion via `chgSpan`), no interval, verdict or run count, in the panel, its tooltips or the hero; one change card with a Chart toggle (Bar or Forest, one metric pick for both); the trade-off, above the version chart; every version on one chart (the leaders over time, only for a steered run); the table (All versions), in the style of the Web UI's Versions table: a sticky Version column with a purple "Best" badge on Artemis's Best (`--accent` border, `--accent-text` text), right-aligned numbers in the page's own face with tabular figures (one sans family, as the checker requires), with the card scrolling it sideways only if a cell ever outgrows it, each change in its direction's colour (`chgSpan`) beside its verdict sign and word, zebra rows and a hover from tokens, fixed column widths (`TABLE_COLS`), and a long title cut with its full text on hover. Below a 1,000px window, where those columns no longer fit beside the rail, it drops What changed and Commit (shown under each version) and lays out to fit, so it never runs wider than its card. Box plots of every iteration get a rail item only when two or more versions have 5 or more iterations (`--only box` still draws them).
- Each Conclusion keeps three bullets at most, so a card puts its most important three first.
- **Conclusion boxes** have an Artemis purple border (`--accent`). A change they state, and every written line that states one (the headline, the one-line lede, verdict lists, "Surest gain"), has its number green when better and red when worse (`chgSpan`, in the verdict text tokens, with the line's own words when it has them); one that rounds to 0.0% reads "unchanged from the original", in neither colour (`roundsToZero`), and a version's result reads as falcon's verdict, else "better than the original" or "worse than the original" with "(1 run each)" or ", no verdict" (`outcome`), one phrase on every card.
- **Best's halo:** wherever Artemis's Best has a mark (the trade-off's two views and the leaders-over-time chart), a soft brand halo (`--halo`) sits behind it, with its label, so colour alone never marks it.
- **Look:** one token block holds every colour and timing (the chart visual spec, on artemis-ui develop f6d00737 `src/styles.css`): brand purple, slate neutrals, the platform's success, destructive and warning verdicts (one step deeper in light where a floor fails), card-coloured plot surface, washes at 13% light and 18% dark (`--wash`, a step above the spec's 10% and 14%), and spec motion (hover 120 ms, highlight 200 ms, entry 320 ms, bars 480 ms by row, reduced motion none, no transition on a theme switch). `test_no_colour_or_timing_outside_the_token_blocks` holds the page to it. In light the card is slate-50 (`--card: #f8fafc`) on a slate-100 page (`--bg: #f1f5f9`), off the spec's white card so the charts are less bright; dark is the spec's. Print keeps white. A metric's name on a chart axis is 14px (`AXIS_NAME_PX`), a step above the 13px ticks.
- Best (the Composite pick) is marked only on the headline, the Best card, the version chart's Overall score view, the box plots (a solid purple box with a thicker outline and a "Best" label) and the All versions table (a "Best" badge), never elsewhere on one metric's chart, where another version can lead.

- Brand, only outside the charts: the Artemis mark and "Made with Artemis Discovery" beside the run name, a TurinTech Artemis badge (mark plus wordmark) by "Open in Artemis", and a 16px mark in a saved PNG or SVG's bottom-right corner, below everything drawn. The mark and wordmark are the Artemis Design System's own files (claude-design `assets/artemis-logo-mark.png` and `assets/logotype.svg`), inlined; the wordmark takes the text colour in both themes. No logos, patterns or banners inside a plot.

## Version chart

- Drawn only when the run has steers (the collector's `steering.points`); a run never steered leaves it out completely, and `--only time` refuses with the reason.
- Its title answers "did the search keep finding better versions, and when did it stop?", e.g. "3 new leaders, the last at v36; none in the 4 measured versions since" (a version with no reading yet is not counted).
- Two views: Mean (first), each version's measured mean of one metric in its own values, and Overall score. No % change view.
- The original is a line at its mean, with a legend entry that hides it; hidden, the axis fits the versions alone.
- The leader so far and each new leader are read from the collector's `runningBest` (a metric with no stored direction has none). New leaders are numbered #1, #2, with no claim of significance: no new leader is called clear of, or within noise of, another.
- A version with no settled verdict is a plain dot named "Version": the chart and its Conclusion never say "needs more runs". Steers are not drawn or quoted here.
- The metric's leader is ringed and labelled "Top"; the good direction is printed in the metric's own way ("↓ lower is better"), never a bare "higher is better" beside a lower-is-better metric.
- On a phone (under 560px): no leader numbers (the title and tooltips still say them), the Top ring stays inside the plot, and its label goes on the side that covers no dot, or is left out.

## Trade-off

- The two metrics are the collector's `tradeoffMetrics`; `build_studio.py --tradeoff X,Y` sets the pair it opens on. The Plot views come from the collector's contract, never from a metric's name:
  - **Values**, first for every pair (the original sits at its real values; at 0% on a % view a reader takes it for a size of 0): both metrics in their own units, the front as a staircase, the original as a triangle, references as diamonds or dashed lines, and the cap line on the budget axis. "Better on both" sits in the corner each metric's direction gives (two lower-is-better metrics: down and left).
  - **Corner washes**, on Values and % change, as on the HF model cards (`corner_wash` in the model-card charts): a soft green wash fading out from the corner that is better on both, a soft red one from the corner worse on both, with no hard edge (radial gradients in the plot's box, radius 0.95 of each side). Coloured by the theme's `--better` and `--worse` tokens in both themes, drawn before every mark and taking no pointer, so no point, label or tooltip is covered. "Better on both" sits in that corner only when no marker is there; with one, a top corner moves it just above the plot and a bottom corner leaves it out (`cornerSpot`). On a phone only the front and Best are labelled; every other point is named in its tooltip.
  - **Within the cap**, second, when one axis is the run's budget metric (`metrics[].role: "budget"`, a stated size cap): size is a budget to spend, not a goal. Each version on the other metric, best first, against the original and each reference; size as budget used (in the budget metric's own unit, and % of the cap) beside each row and in the tooltip; versions over the cap grey.
  - **% change**, last: Artemis's % change on both, each axis in its metric's own direction (falcon's % better with the sign turned for a lower-is-better metric, so a model 12.6% larger plots at +12.6%), and each axis tagged "higher is better" or "lower is better". The green wash and "Better on both" sit in the corner the two directions give, the red wash in the opposite one, as in Values. The original is labelled "no change on either". The axes keep 0 in view and fit every version that passed the gate; there is no zoom. On and behind the front are the collector's stored front, as in Values.
- **Markers, one scheme on every trade-off and Pareto view:** the Best (Artemis's pick) a solid purple triangle (`--accent`) with its halo; every other version an open purple triangle (accent outline, card fill), faded when behind the front; the original a grey triangle (`--orig`, slate-800 light, slate-400 dark, artemis-ui styles.css :138 and :134), never orange; a gate failure its red cross. Legend swatches draw exactly these. The first chart's original bar stays the neutral bar colour.
- **One trade-off card per page:** the trade-off card whenever the run has two measured metrics to draw it (`tradeoffMetrics`); the Pareto card (the same Trade-off rail item) only for a run where it can't be drawn, such as one measured metric against an AI Metric. `--only pareto` refuses a run that has the trade-off card.
- **No interval marks on any trade-off or Pareto view:** no shaded boxes and no whiskers or crosses behind the triangles; a version's 95% interval is in its tooltip, and the forest and the bars draw intervals.
- Every front is the collector's (`fronts`, read with `frontOf()`): its one dominance rule on falcon's means, at least as good on both and better on one, with no claim of significance. A pair with no stored front calls no version on or behind one. The Pareto card draws gate failures as red crosses, hidden at first, at the edge with an arrow when a reading is past the axes, as the trade-off does. The page works out no front; hiding a version leaves the front as stored. The front's staircase is drawn through the stored front and turns at the corner both neighbours beat (`stairCorner()`), so it follows each axis's direction too.
- References are only the collector's `externalReferences` (`--reference`): a point where the file has both readings, a dashed line where it has one, "Same size: ..." when its budget reading is the cap, red and "fails the gate (...)" when it fails the run's gate.
- Versions that failed the gate (`gate`) start hidden in every view; their legend entry shows struck through, and one click brings them back as red crosses, drawn at the edge when a reading is past the axes. They never set an axis on any card.
- "1 run each, so no significance" is said only when every reading behind the view is one run against one.
- Every legend mark is the chart's own shape at legend size, and everything drawn has a legend entry. Best is always labelled ("v40 · Best", or "v40 · Top" when Artemis gives no Best). The axis menus and Plot are the card's one row of controls (`.ctls`): one caption size, one 34px height, aligned captions and boxes; on a phone the row takes its own line and wraps control by control.

## Other cards

- **Measured once** (no 95% interval on any version, decided from the snapshot): no Forest toggle or card. With some intervals, the cards draw those.
- **Ranked bars: verdict colour, else direction** (pretty charts, different from the Artemis UI): a bar with falcon's verdict takes its colour, green significantly improved, amber no significant difference, red significantly regressed, with the verdict word. A bar with no verdict (one run, falcon's pending, or no comparison) is solid green when falcon's % better is above 0 and red below, with "▲ better" or "▼ worse" beside it; exactly 0 stays neutral, with "= no change" ("≈" is only falcon's no significant difference). No dashed or neutral outline bars. The legend lists only the keys drawn: the verdicts, then "Better than the original" and "Worse than the original" tagged "(1 run each)" or ", no verdict". The Conclusion counts the verdicts, then "N better, M worse than the original" for the bars with none. The sessions card's bars follow the same rule.
- **Verdicts elsewhere are falcon's or none:** on the other cards, when every reading behind a card is one run against one, falcon's pending reads "1 run each, so no significance", never a better or worse of the page's own. The page never says two versions "can't be told apart" or counts versions that "beat" the original or a reference from the means. A Conclusion line that would say nothing is left out, never drawn as an empty bullet.
- **Gate failures elsewhere:** the ranked, forest and box cards leave them out with a "Failed the gate" line; the version chart draws a cross; the table says "failed the gate, values not usable".
- **Table:** verdict signs ✓ ○ ≈ ▼ with a key, "Δ% vs original" with its 95% interval in brackets; versions that failed to run or were not measured sit behind a counted "Show failed versions (N)".
- **Tooltips**, on every card but the first: version and experiment title; the change vs the original with falcon's 95% CI and verdict; its rank on the metric; the measured mean with its unit and iterations; then the experiment's outcome in grey. A card adds only its own lines after these. The tests read tooltips by hovering forest and trade-off points: a bar chart's tooltip follows the pointer's position, which headless Chrome does not synthesise, so the ranked bars' own lines (such as a bar's "▲ better") are checked by eye.
- **Hiding and selecting versions are shared:** two lists per run (or session), both cleared by Reset. Hidden (`offV`): unticking a version under any card's Versions, or a table row's box, hides it on every card at once; the table keeps the row, dimmed. Selected (`focusV`): clicking a version's bar, point, forest row, leaders-chart dot or axis label, or Enter or Space on it, or clicking its name in the table, selects it on every card; several can be selected, and a second click clears one. While any is selected, every card emphasises them and dims the rest to `--dim`, the table marks the row with an accent bar, and each card shows a "Clear selection" button. Legend entries hide a series on their own card only.
- **Links:** a version's tooltip (on hover or keyboard focus of its bar, point or forest row) ends with "Open in Artemis", which opens that version's Code tab (new tab). The tooltip stays open while the pointer moves into it; Tab from the focused mark goes to the link, Escape closes it. The marks themselves select, never navigate. The link is built from `run.webUrl` and the version's `id`; the footer's "Open in Artemis" uses the same URL, else `run.projectUrl`. Links go only under the run's own Web UI origin, `run.webOrigin`: `<origin>/projects/<id>/discover/<run>` (or `/projects/<id>`), and nothing else. A snapshot with no `webOrigin` has no links.

## Look and motion

- A change label ("29.8% lower") is coloured by good or bad in the metric's own direction, never by up or down, on the forest rows: green or red from falcon's verdict only, and there no significant difference and needs more runs stay neutral. The Best card, every written line and the All versions table colour a change by the sign of falcon's change instead, through `chgSpan`: a change falcon calls within noise is still green or red by its direction, and in the table falcon's verdict word sits beside it. The ranked bars themselves take the verdict colour, or green or red by direction when there is no verdict (see Other cards); their change label keeps this rule. The verdict word, sign or the bar's side of 0 always says it too. Light mode deepens the verdict shade (#166534, #b91c1c).
- Cards fade and rise in as they first scroll into view, under 600ms (bars grow, trend dots pop in order, Best's changes count up, the Bar / Forest switch morphs); never under prefers-reduced-motion or in print.
- Box plots tint every other column with the theme's subtle surface (`--subtle`), full height; never a continuous scatter. They carry no verdict colours (the forest does): the original grey (`--orig`), every version purple (`--accent`), Artemis's Best solid purple (`--accent-text`) with a thicker outline and a "Best" label.
- A box is Artemis's quartiles and median with whiskers from the lowest to the highest run, and no per-run dots over or around it (they hid the box). Only a group with no box (under 5 runs, or a machine's pooled runs) shows its runs as dots, with its mean. The card's ⓘ says so.
- "Download PDF" opens the print dialog in the light theme, without the controls. Charts download as PNG, SVG or CSV.
- The page inlines its data but loads its pinned chart libraries from cdnjs and unpkg, so it needs the network to draw.
